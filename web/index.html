How it works

  1. 01 Paste a link, or drop a fileA YouTube URL, or an .mp4/.mkv/.mov recording — optionally with its own .vtt/.srt transcript attached.
  2. 02 We get a transcriptReuses YouTube's own captions or your uploaded transcript when there is one; otherwise transcribes and identifies speakers itself.
  3. 03 An AI editor marks it upReal sentences, not caption fragments, are judged keep or cut against the mode you chose.
  4. 04 ffmpeg cuts exactly thatA keyframe-aware cut and concat, with a marked-up transcript showing what made it in.
