Meetings are where the work happens. Nobody wants to watch them twice.

{{ app_name }} puts the moments worth learning from first, then plays the meeting without the noise.

Built at FloLabs, for our own recordings.

{# OWNER: this is a factual starting point; replace or extend it with your own words. #}

At FloLabs we record our product syncs, architecture reviews and demos. Those recordings were the only complete record of what was decided and explained, and almost nobody watched them. An hour of meeting holds a few minutes that matter, surrounded by greetings, crosstalk and “can you see my screen?”.

Cutting them by hand meant scrubbing through timelines. So we flipped the problem around. The transcript already says everything that was said. An editor can mark up text in minutes, and a machine can cut a video exactly where the text says.

{{ app_name }} is that idea taken seriously: an AI copy editor that judges every sentence, a frame-exact renderer that cuts precisely what it marked, and the outputs people use, from a highlights reel to vertical shorts and a report of everything that was removed.

How we build it

  • The transcript is the edit

    Decisions are made on text, where they are fast to make and easy to check. Every cut has a reason you can read, and the removed parts stay available.

  • Frame-exact, not roughly right

    Cuts land on the source frame grid and the audio is assembled to the sample, so the video, the transcript and the chapters always agree.

  • Cheap by design

    The AI reads text in compact batches with a budget model, about two cents for a 98-minute meeting. Rendering runs on a small CPU server.

  • Your recordings stay yours

    Videos are processed on the server that runs {{ app_name }}. Only transcript text goes to the AI provider, and deleting a job deletes everything it made.

Under the hood: FastAPI, ffmpeg, yt-dlp for YouTube, OpenAI or Gemini for reading the transcript, and SQLite or Postgres for accounts.
