The story

Built at FloLabs, for our own recordings.

{# OWNER: this is a factual starting point; replace or extend it with your own words. #}

At FloLabs we record our product syncs, architecture reviews and demos. The recordings were the only complete record of decisions and explanations, and almost nobody watched them: an hour of meeting holds a few minutes that matter, surrounded by greetings, crosstalk and "can you see my screen?".

Cutting them by hand meant scrubbing through timelines. So we flipped the problem: the transcript already says everything that was said. An editor can mark up text in minutes, and a machine can cut a video exactly where the text says.

{{ app_name }} is that idea taken seriously: an AI copy editor that judges every sentence, a frame-exact renderer that cuts precisely what it marked, and the outputs people actually use, from a highlights reel to vertical shorts and a report of everything that was removed.

How we build it

Four principles

{{ icon("file") }}

The transcript is the edit

Decisions are made on text, where they are fast to make and easy to check. Every cut has a reason you can read, and the removed parts stay available.

{{ icon("scissors") }}

Frame-exact, not roughly right

Cuts land on the source's frame grid and the audio is assembled to the sample, so the video, the transcript and the chapters always agree.

{{ icon("cpu") }}

Cheap by design

The AI reads text in compact batches with a budget model: about two cents for a 98-minute meeting. Rendering runs on a small CPU server.

{{ icon("lock") }}

Your recordings stay yours

Videos are processed on the server you run. Only transcript text goes to the AI provider, and you can delete a job and everything it made at any time.
