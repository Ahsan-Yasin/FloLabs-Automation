{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the edit #}

The transcript is the edit.

No timeline and no scrubbing. An AI editor marks up every sentence the way a copy editor would, and ffmpeg cuts exactly what it marked.

  1. Bring a recordingA YouTube link or a file from any meeting tool. Captions, or a .vtt or .srt you already have, are reused.
  2. An AI editor marks it upEach sentence is kept or cut, with a reason. You can say in plain words what counts as a highlight.
  3. ffmpeg cuts exactly thatCuts land on the source frame grid, kept parts meet with a short dissolve, and long silences shrink to a beat.
{# ---------------------------------------------------------------- what comes back #}

Everything comes back in one zip.

Download it from the dashboard, or let your automation fetch it when the webhook arrives.

  • final.mp4
    The highlights reel, a title card, then the cleaned meeting.
  • shorts/
    Vertical 1080×1920 clips with burned-in captions, ready to post.
  • removed.mp4
    Every cut of a second or more, labelled with its original time and the reason.
  • report.pdf
    What was removed and why, the highlights, the shorts and the chapters. Easy to forward.
  • transcript_clean.txt
    What the final video says, with its own timestamps. The removed text comes alongside, as text and JSON.
  • chapters.txt
    YouTube chapters for the final video, ready to paste into the description.

# Cut a YouTube video and get a webhook when it's done
curl -X POST {{ base_url }}/api/v1/jobs/youtube \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HC_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "url": "https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID",
    "options": { "shorts_count": 4 },
    "callback_url": "https://n8n.example.com/webhook/cut"
  }'
import os, time, httpx
api = httpx.Client(base_url="{{ base_url }}/api/v1",
                   headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['HC_API_KEY']}"})
job = api.post("/jobs/youtube", json={"url": "https://youtu.be/..."}).json()
while job["status"] not in ("done", "failed"):
    time.sleep(30)
    job = api.get(f"/jobs/{job['job_id']}").json()
open("meeting.zip", "wb").write(api.get(f"/jobs/{job['job_id']}/bundle").content)
